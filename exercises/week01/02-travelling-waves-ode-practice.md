# Week 1 Day 1 Exercises

## Exercise 2: Travelling Waves and ODE Practice

### Question 1: Wave parameters

Consider the travelling wave

$$
u_1(x,t)=3\cos\left(20t-5x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right).
$$

Determine:

1. the propagation direction;
2. the angular frequency $\omega$;
3. the phase constant $\beta$;
4. the phase velocity $v_p$;
5. the wavelength $\lambda$;
6. the frequency $f$.

### Question 2: Propagation direction using constant phase

Determine the propagation direction of

$$
u_2(x,t)=2\sin(4x+12t).
$$

Use the constant-phase method to justify the answer. Also identify $\omega$ and $\beta$.

### Question 3: Oscillatory ODE

Find the general solution of

$$
y''+16y=0
$$

in both forms:

1. trigonometric form;
2. complex-exponential form.

### Question 4: Boundary condition and exponential solutions

Consider

$$
y''-4y=0.
$$

Find the general solution. If $y(x)$ must remain finite as $x\to+\infty$, determine which term must be removed and state the resulting condition on its coefficient.
