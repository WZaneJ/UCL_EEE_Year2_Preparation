# Week 1 Day 1 Exercises

## Exercise 1: Diagnostic Test

### Question 1: Complex-number operations

Let

$$
z_1=3+4j,
\qquad
z_2=1-2j.
$$

Calculate:

1. $|z_1|$
2. $z_1^*$
3. $z_1z_2$
4. $z_1/z_2$

### Question 2: Exponential form

Write

$$
z=-1+j\sqrt{3}
$$

in exponential and polar forms.

### Question 3: Euler's formula

Simplify:

$$
e^{j\pi/3}+e^{-j\pi/3}
$$

and

$$
e^{j\pi/3}-e^{-j\pi/3}.
$$

### Question 4: Propagation direction

Determine the propagation directions of:

$$
E_1(z,t)=E_0\cos(\omega t-\beta z)
$$

and

$$
E_2(z,t)=E_0\cos(\omega t+\beta z).
$$

### Question 5: ODEs

Find the general solutions of:

$$
y''+9y=0
$$

$$
y''-9y=0
$$

$$
y''+6y'+9y=0.
$$
